# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in **ASLytics** a harassment-free and inclusive experience for everyone, with special commitment to accessibility and respect for Deaf, DeafBlind, Hard-of-Hearing, CODA, and multilingual community members.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* Respecting diverse communication modalities (American Sign Language, regional sign languages, written gloss, captioned speech, and multilingual text).
* Designing interfaces that avoid audio-only assumptions or inaccessible contrast.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a confidential issue or contacting the repository maintainers.
